## Status meanings

- **Needs review:** A lead exists, but I haven't checked enough evidence yet.
- **Suspected:** Specific evidence raises concern, but it isn't conclusive.
- **Corroborated:** Multiple reliable sources support the concern.
- **Confirmed by source:** A trustworthy source explicitly verifies the finding. Name and link that source.
- **Inconclusive / cleared:** Evidence is insufficient, conflicting, or points away from the initial suspicion.
